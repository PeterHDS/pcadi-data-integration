# Interpretation scope

- OCS records submissions received through contributing online systems; other contact routes require their own evidence.
- GPAD records scheduled or planned appointment-system activity; workload, capacity and unmet demand require additional evidence.
- CBT coverage is supplier- and participation-dependent and includes mapping limitations for accounts serving multiple practices.
- Aggregate source files preserve submissions, appointments and calls as separate records without patient-level linkage.
- Separate source totals remain distinct operational measures throughout the pipeline.
- Temporal correspondence describes co-occurring patterns; channel substitution and causal effects require a separate study design.
- Rates per 1,000 registered patient-months normalise recorded activity by practice list exposure; access quality requires additional evidence.
- Matched cohorts are selected populations and can differ systematically from practices excluded by source availability.
- Annual profiles exclude practices without complete twelve-month evidence.
- The April 2025 Y60 CBT day/time gap remains missing and explicitly flagged in the reference temporal output.

## Analytical safeguards

- Source absence retains its documented status and remains distinct from observed zero activity.
- OCS, GPAD and CBT counts remain separate source-specific measures.
- Integration outputs preserve individual features and populations rather than creating an access score or practice ranking.
- Every geographic value retains its published organisational grain.
- Comparisons use explicitly aligned observation periods.
- Matched-source results carry their reporting population and coverage evidence.
