# PCADI v1.0.0 — Dissertation Reference Release

PCADI v1.0.0 is the executable and documentary companion to the DS7010
dissertation. It publishes the integration logic, fixed reference outputs and
validation evidence used to construct national and telephony-restricted
primary-care activity profiles.

## Reproducibility evidence

- official-source manifests distinguish publication vintage from observation
  month;
- source tables are reduced independently to one practice-month row before
  joining;
- every join uses practice code and reporting month;
- a union practice-month spine preserves source coverage and absence;
- annual rates use the sum of matched monthly registered-patient denominators;
- annual shares use annual component totals rather than averages of monthly
  percentages;
- missing practice-months are not converted to zero;
- deterministic tests cover one, three, twelve and twenty-four months; and
- reference outputs are locked by dimensions, schemas and SHA-256 checksums.

The [analytical contract and lineage](../audits/ANALYTICAL_CONTRACT_AND_LINEAGE.md)
and [validation gate](../../validation/repository_release_gate.csv) provide the
technical evidence for review.

## Scope

PCADI integrates recorded activity from different systems. OCS submissions,
GPAD appointments and CBT calls remain separate measures. The outputs do not
establish total demand, access quality, patient-level linkage or causal channel
substitution.
