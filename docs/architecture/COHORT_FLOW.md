# Reference-application cohort flow

![The annual OCS-GPAD matrix contains two nested CBT evidence-availability cohorts](../assets/pcadi-cohort-flow.svg)

The April 2025 to March 2026 annual outputs use explicit evidence-availability
rules:

1. OCS and GPAD practice-month inputs are validated independently.
2. Annual eligibility requires twelve complete matched months.
3. This produces 6,067 practices in the national annual matrix.
4. Valid CBT inbound evidence identifies a nested cohort of 3,020 practices.
5. Complete supported CBT outcome fields identify a further nested cohort of
   1,456 practices.

The smaller populations are not sequential quality grades. They reflect which
additional CBT measures are available under each analytical contract. Every
shared feature is inherited exactly from the parent matrix after alignment by
standardised practice code.

See the [reference application](../reference-applications/apr2025-mar2026.md)
and [analytical contract](../audits/ANALYTICAL_CONTRACT_AND_LINEAGE.md) for the
complete eligibility and validation rules.
