# April 2025 to March 2026 verified reference application

This page documents the official prepared data and frozen outputs included with PCADI. It is one verified application of the reusable pipeline.

## Observation period

The analytical window covers observations from 1 April 2025 through 31 March
2026. Publication files released later may supply or revise observations inside that window; the publication month is recorded separately from the observation month.

## Included sources

The application uses selected official NHS England publications for:

- Online Consultation Systems (OCS);
- General Practice Appointments Data (GPAD);
- Cloud Based Telephony (CBT); and
- registered-patient counts carried by the validated monthly source contract.

ODS practice codes provide the organisational identity used for linkage. Activity measures originate from their respective publication families. The exact selected publication members, release vintages, observation-month ownership and source checksums are in [`reference-release/manifests`](../../reference-release/manifests) and the [source register](../../validation/authoritative_source_register.csv).

## Preparation boundary

Official raw downloads remain available from their publishers and large working databases remain in the governed local workspace. The repository contains complete SQL, prepared-source contracts, source manifests, compact outputs, data dictionaries and validation registers.

The full source reconstruction requires the 21 exact selected source CSVs. The compact validation route checks frozen outputs without rebuilding the large source database.

## Annual matrices

| Matrix | Rows | Columns | Numerical features | Purpose |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `primary_practice_access_clustering_matrix.csv` | 6,067 | 15 | 14 | National annual OCS-GPAD reference matrix |
| `cbt_inbound_sensitivity_clustering_matrix_17_features.csv` | 3,020 | 18 | 17 | CBT inbound evidence sensitivity cohort |
| `cbt_outcomes_sensitivity_clustering_matrix_21_features.csv` | 1,456 | 22 | 21 | CBT outcome-complete sensitivity cohort |

Every matrix contains one unique standardised practice code and complete finite numerical modelling values. The identifier is retained for traceability and excluded from the numerical feature matrix.

The GPAD booking-delay composition retains separate `same day`, `1 day`, `2 to 7 days`, `8 to 14 days` and `over 14 days` features. The 3,020-practice cohort is nested inside the 6,067-practice matrix. The 1,456-practice cohort is nested inside both. Shared parent values are inherited exactly.

## Validation

Run:

```powershell
python automation/pipeline_cli.py validate-reference `
  --restore-missing `
  --output work/reference_validation.csv
```

This checks all 14 registered reference outputs. If release-only files are absent, the command retrieves `PCADI_REFERENCE_OUTPUTS_APR2025_MAR2026.zip` from the `reference-apr2025-mar2026` release, verifies its exact size and SHA-256 checksum, restores `outputs/<filename>` members and validates each CSV.

Complete file fingerprints are recorded in the [authoritative output manifest](../../validation/authoritative_output_manifest.csv). Feature definitions are in the [feature dictionary](../../reference-release/documentation/FEATURE_DICTIONARY.md). The [analytical contract and lineage](../audits/ANALYTICAL_CONTRACT_AND_LINEAGE.md) documents annual formulas, cohort rules and inherited-value checks.

## Analytical use

The annual matrices are prepared inputs for downstream analysis. Integration and reference validation end at the validated matrix contract. A downstream specification records its modelling method, preprocessing, uncertainty and sensitivity analysis.

## Interpretation scope

The outputs describe recorded practice activity, reporting coverage and source availability. OCS, GPAD and CBT remain separate measures. Missing records preserve their source-specific meaning, while observed zero values retain their reported meaning. Patient journeys, performance, total demand, workload, causal effects and channel substitution require additional data or study designs.

The reference application supported an MSc research project. Its academic role and examiner route are documented in [Research context](../research-context.md). PCADI can be reused independently of that study.
